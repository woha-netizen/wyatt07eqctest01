About this demo

A small application that shows how identity and access management fits together, built on Cloudflare Workers and D1.

What IAM covers

Identification
Saying who you are. Here that is a username or email.
Authentication
Proving it. Here that is your password.
Authorisation
Deciding what you may do. Here that is your role: member or administrator.
Accountability
Keeping a record. Here that is the activity log.

What happens when you sign in

  • Your password is combined with a random salt and hashed with PBKDF2 (SHA-256). The password itself is never stored.
  • The server creates a random session token, keeps only its hash, and sends the token to your browser in a cookie that scripts cannot read.
  • Every protected page and every API call checks that session on the server before answering.
  • Five failed attempts lock the account for ten minutes, and each attempt is written to the activity log.

What is stored

  • Your username, email, password hash, role and status.
  • One session record per signed-in device, with a device name and IP address, so you can sign devices out.
  • An activity log of sign-ins and account changes, kept for 90 days.

Limits of a demo

  • There is no email verification, password reset by email or multi-factor sign-in.
  • For production use, put Cloudflare Access in front of the application and use a single sign-on provider.
