Help

Answers to the questions people ask most.

How do I create an account?

Choose Register, enter a username, an email and a password of at least 10 characters. You are signed in straight away. The very first account on a new install becomes the administrator.

Can I sign in with my username instead of my email?

Yes. The sign-in form accepts either one, and neither is case sensitive.

I forgot my password. What now?

This demo cannot send email, so there is no reset link. Ask an administrator. They can delete the old account so you can register again, or reset it directly in the database.

Why was my account locked?

Five wrong passwords in a row lock an account for ten minutes. The attempts appear on your Activity page. If you did not make them, change your password once you are back in.

How do I sign out a device I no longer use?

Open Security. Every signed-in device is listed there, and you can sign out one device or all the others. Changing your password signs out every other device too.

Why can I not see the Admin page?

It is only for administrators. If you open it without that role you see an access denied page. Ask an administrator to change your role.

What does an administrator see about me?

Your username, email, role, status and last sign-in, plus the activity log. Administrators never see your password.
